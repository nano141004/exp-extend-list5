\section{Results}

Because the current setup evaluates only 50 queries per dataset, the results should be interpreted as preliminary evidence rather than a full benchmark. The local baseline is sequential ListT5 with BM25 top-100 under the same 50-query setting.

Table~\ref{tab:ndcg-results} reports the effectiveness results using NDCG@10. The sequential grouping columns compare different BM25 candidate budgets, while the score-balanced column compares the proposed grouping strategy against sequential grouping at BM25 top-100.

\begin{table*}[t]
  \centering
  \begin{tabular}{lccccc}
    \toprule
    & \multicolumn{4}{c}{Sequential grouping} & \multicolumn{1}{c}{Score-balanced} \\
    \cmidrule(lr){2-5} \cmidrule(lr){6-6}
    Dataset & Top-40 & Top-60 & Top-80 & Top-100 & Top-100 \\
    \midrule
    NFCorpus & 0.33718 & \textbf{0.34292} & 0.34212 & 0.34274 & 0.33855 \\
    SciFact & 0.72975 & 0.72829 & \textbf{0.73023} & 0.72683 & 0.72262 \\
    ArguAna & \textbf{0.46052} & 0.44007 & 0.41867 & 0.43436 & 0.41691 \\
    SCIDOCS & 0.16004 & 0.15644 & \textbf{0.17279} & 0.16678 & 0.16164 \\
    FiQA-2018 & 0.41760 & 0.43894 & \textbf{0.45958} & 0.45442 & 0.45446 \\
    \bottomrule
  \end{tabular}
  \caption{NDCG@10 on the first 50 queries of each dataset. Bold marks the highest score in each row.}
  \label{tab:ndcg-results}
\end{table*}

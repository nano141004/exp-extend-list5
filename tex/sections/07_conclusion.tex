\section{Conclusion}
\label{sec:conclusion}

We presented a small inference-only study of ListT5-base under a 50-query evaluation setup.
The current results suggest that reducing BM25 top-k can be competitive with sequential top-100: the best NDCG@10 setting varies by dataset, and top-60 is the fastest setting across all five datasets.
For the grouping experiment, score-balanced top-100 does not show a clear quality improvement over sequential top-100. 
The strategy-aware caching update is still useful for efficiency, reducing score-balanced runtime by roughly a factor of two.
Overall, the strongest immediate signal is that BM25 top-k deserves careful tuning before scaling the grouping comparison to full-query evaluation.
